"""Computes volume fractions for the points that are consistent with the points being the cell centroids of a partition of the domain."""

Cells that tile the domain must satisfy two conservation identities. The sum of their volumes is the domain volume, and the volume-weighted sum of their centroids is the domain volume times the domain centroid. This node takes the input points as the target centroids and finds fractions `f_i > 0` with `sum f_i = 1` and `sum f_i P_i = c_D`. Here `c_D` is the domain centroid.

Of all such fractions, it returns the maximum-entropy one. Like maximum-entropy coordinates, it minimizes `sum f_i log f_i` under these constraints. The solve is a small convex problem in `d + 1` unknowns, done with Newton's method on the dual: `min_y sum_i exp(-y . l_i) + y . l_D`, with `l_i = [P_i, 1]`.

Fractions exist exactly when the domain centroid lies inside the convex hull of the points. Otherwise the node reports an error.

The output attribute can be used directly as the targets of [PP Power Diagram Solver|Node:sop/pp_powerdiagramsolver].

@inputs
    Sites:
        Points used as the target cell centroids. In 2D they are projected onto the chosen plane.

    Clipping Domain:
        2D: triangles in the chosen plane. 3D: tetrahedra. Only the domain's centroid and volume are used.

@outputs
    Sites:
        The input points, with these attributes added:
        - the output attribute (point, float): the volume fractions;
        - `domain_volume` (detail, float).

@parameters
    == Main ==

    Mode:
        #id: mode
        2D: the domain is a triangle mesh in one of the axis planes. 3D: the domain is a tet mesh.

    Plane:
        #id: plane
        (2D only) Plane holding the domain and the points.

    Output Attribute:
        #id: outattrib
        Float point attribute where the fractions are written.

    == Newton Solver ==

    Tolerance:
        #id: tolerance
        Converged when the conservation identities hold to this tolerance, measured relative to the size of the points' bounding box.

    Max Iterations, Regularization, Max Step Halvings, Print Iterations:
        Damped Newton with a backtracking line search, as in [PP Power Diagram Solver|Node:sop/pp_powerdiagramsolver].
