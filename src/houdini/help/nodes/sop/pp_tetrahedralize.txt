"""Fills the volume inside a closed triangulated surface with tetrahedra. The result is the 3D domain for PP Power Diagram."""

Fills the volume bounded by the surface with tetrahedra, using TetGen through geogram's `mesh_tetrahedralize`. The tets follow the input surface exactly: every surface triangle is a face of a tet.

The volume is everything the surface encloses, so a closed shell inside another one becomes a cavity. Triangle winding is ignored. Output tets always have positive volume.

@inputs
    Closed Triangulated Surface:
        Triangles only (use a Divide SOP). When __Preprocess Surface__ is off, the surface must also be (not checked):
        - welded, so neighboring triangles share points (use a Fuse SOP);
        - closed and manifold: every edge is shared by exactly two triangles;
        - free of self-intersections and zero-area triangles.

        It may be non-convex, have several pieces, or enclose cavities.

@outputs
    Tetrahedra:
        Tetrahedron primitives on welded points. Unless the surface was preprocessed, the input points come first, in their original order. With __Refine__ on, the points TetGen adds come after them. Input attributes aren't carried over.

@parameters
    Preprocess Surface:
        #id: preprocess
        Tries to repair the surface first: merges duplicate points, fills small holes, resolves self-intersections and keeps only the outer boundary. Point positions and numbers can change.

    Refine:
        #id: refine
        Adds points inside the volume to improve tet quality. When off, the tets only use the surface points.

    Quality:
        #id: quality
        (Refine only) Target radius-edge ratio, usually between 1 and 2. Lower values give better-shaped tets, and more of them.

@related
    - [Node:sop/pp_powerdiagram]
